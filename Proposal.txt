US Wind Farms will follow this example: 

        "name": 
        "capacity_mw": 
        "turbine_mw": 
        "num_turbines": 
        "depth_m": 
        "distance_to_shore_m": 

Example:
        "name": "Vineyard Wind 1",
        "capacity_mw": 806,
        "turbine_mw": 13,
        "num_turbines": 62,
        "depth_m": -42,
        "distance_to_shore_m": 42139
